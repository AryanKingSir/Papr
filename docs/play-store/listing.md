## Full description (4000 max)

```
Papr turns paper into clean, searchable PDFs, and your documents stay on your phone.

SCAN IN SECONDS
• Point your camera and Papr finds the page edges, straightens it, and cleans it up
• Scan many pages into one document, add pages later, or rescan a single page
• Import photos of documents from your gallery

FIND ANYTHING
• Text recognition (OCR) makes the words in your scans searchable
• Search by title or by the text inside a scan
• Filter by date to find last month's receipts fast

SIGN AND MARK UP
• Draw your signature once, then drop it on any page, drag it into place, and resize it
• Pen, highlighter, text notes, and an eraser, with undo and redo
• Black out confidential details. The marks are burned into the exported page, so the hidden text really is gone

ORGANISE
• Reorder, rotate, and delete pages in a simple grid
• Merge several documents into one
• Rename documents and see them all at a glance

SHARE THE WAY YOU NEED
• Export as PDF or as JPG images
• Add a searchable text layer to your PDFs
• Protect a PDF with a password
• Shrink a PDF to a target size for email or upload forms

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no ads
• Scanning and text recognition run on your device
• Your documents are never uploaded. They only leave your phone when you share them

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL ONE-TIME UPGRADE
Scanning, signing, markup, search, and plain PDF and image exports are free with no limits. Each month you also get 3 free uses each of searchable-text exports, password-protected exports, compressed exports, and merges.
Papr Pro is a single one-time purchase, with no subscription. It removes every monthly limit and unlocks confidential marking.

Light and dark themes, and Material You colours on Android 12 and newer.
```
